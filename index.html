November Cycle

Coaching Session

Reconnect as a Design Studio community, reflect on your app and a Critical Friend’s app, and run a pressure test to guide your next revision.

Community agreements

  • Stay engaged and curious.
  • Stories stay, learning leaves.
  • Be mindful of airtime and promote equity of voice.
  • Recognize power dynamics. Be aware of your own power and privilege and how to use it.
  • Acknowledge intent, prioritize impact, and keep the conversation going.
  • Treat disagreement as a positive opportunity for learning.
  • Be respectful and mindful of our own and others’ strengths and growing edges.
